Chat Went Crazy Clipper – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Chat Went Crazy Clipper ("the app") is a private publishing tool used only by its operator.

What we collect: when the operator signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token, a refresh token and the account's basic profile (open ID, display name). It does not collect data from any other TikTok user, and has no other users.

How it is used: only to upload and publish the operator's own videos to the operator's own TikTok account.

Storage: tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer in a file readable only by the operator. They are never sold, shared or sent to anyone other than TikTok.

Deletion: revoking the app's access in TikTok (Settings > Security > Apps) and deleting the local token file removes all data.
